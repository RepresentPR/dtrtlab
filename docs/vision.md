## Decision filter

Ask before shipping or adopting a tool:

1. Does it help a local business get a clearer free office map, a Job Desk, or the AI Visibility Fix?
2. Can we show dated evidence without lying?
3. Does it move durable craft or revenue forward, even slowly, without theater?
4. Would we be proud if a local business owner or a journalist read it tomorrow?

If no, or if it only copies someone else’s product in secret, it is out. During the freeze, “make the story world more real” is not a reason to spend company time on AFTERLIGHT, streaming, or `/live/`.

## Explicitly out

- Reverse-engineering or cloning third-party apps as a growth strategy
- Fake headcount, fake clients, fake Fortune or “top 50” claims
- Live store, open raise, or hiring pages for pipelines that do not exist
- Cold spam, fake checkouts, and growth hacks that burn trust
- Treating Stage 1 targets as finished work
