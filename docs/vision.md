# DTRT Lab — personal & company vision (aligned)

**Owner:** Ian Ferraro  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-13  
**Public pages:** [/about.html](https://dtrtlab.com/about.html) · [/company.html](https://dtrtlab.com/company.html)

This is the filter for every product, post, hire, tool, and partnership. If an idea fails this filter, we skip it — even if it is trending.

---

## Personal vision (Ian)

1. **Do the right thing.** Honest status, dated evidence, no fake scale.
2. **Make useful stuff.** Free plans and public work that help a real business or idea take a clearer next step.
3. **Have fun doing it.** Creator craft (stream → edit → publish) stays alive; the Lab is not a joyless corporate shell.
4. **Show the process.** Publish work and results so people can decide whether to collaborate or hire — collision-repair discipline applied to creative work: track details, explain decisions, finish the job.
5. **Help Puerto Rico businesses with future Lab revenue.** No funding program is active yet. When cash exists, the long-term aim is local usefulness — not extractive growth theater.

## Company vision (DTRT Lab)

DTRT Lab is Ian’s independent studio that turns that personal vision into a durable company:

| Pillar | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Usefulness** | Live free plan; paid work only after written scope |
| **Entertainment** | DTRT AFTERLIGHT — Puerto Rico story world, honest “in development” |
| **Evidence** | `/live/` dated dashboard; no vanity metrics without context |
| **Trust** | `@dtrtlab.com` inbox, legal/brand/press pages, no fake hiring/fundraising |
| **Scale with integrity** | Entity → bank → trademarks → store only when real; path toward a top-tier global company without pretending we are one today |
| **Made for all** | Any age, any size idea; EN/ES journey; global audience from Puerto Rico roots |
| **Revenue snowball** | Digital + print-on-demand interest first (`/shop.html`); checkout off until delivery is real — no fake cart |

**North star:** A durable independent entertainment *and* usefulness company — Made for all, global by design — that can eventually fund help for Puerto Rico businesses — not a vanity brand, not a clone shop, not a hype machine.

## Dual brand (intentional)

- **DTRT Lab / free plan / partners** — usefulness for businesses and ideas (any size).
- **DTRTC / clips / AFTERLIGHT** — creator entertainment and original worlds.
- Same founder, same honesty rules. Different surfaces. Neither pretends to be the other.

## Decision filter (use this weekly)

Ask before shipping or adopting a tool:

1. Does it make something **more useful** or the story world **more real**?
2. Can we show **dated evidence** without lying?
3. Does it move **Puerto Rico–aligned** revenue or craft forward (even if slowly)?
4. Would we be proud if a local business owner or a journalist read it tomorrow?

If “no” or “only if we copy someone else’s product in secret” → **out**.

## Explicitly out of vision

- Reverse-engineering or cloning third-party apps to “rebuild without source” (viral RE tooling as a growth strategy)
- Fake headcount, fake clients, fake Fortune / “top 50” claims
- Live store, open raise, or hiring pages that imply pipelines that do not exist
- Growth hacks that burn trust for short-term reach

## In vision right now (priority order)

1. Deliver useful free plans; convert only with clear paid scopes  
2. Grow shop interest (digital packs + POD merch) → flip checkout only when payment + fulfillment are real  
3. Ship AFTERLIGHT chapters / playtest readiness with honest status  
4. Keep public evidence and company foundation tight (legal, brand, press, email)  
5. Register entity + send-as `hello@` when ready  
6. Later: revenue → Puerto Rico business help program (design when cash is real)
