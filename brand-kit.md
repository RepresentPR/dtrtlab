# DTRT Lab brand kit

Public brand for the business site. Last reviewed 2026-10-15 (logo A).

The company name in sentences is **DTRT Lab**. The handle is **@DTRTLab**. Mission: **Do the right thing** — writing for real local businesses, not extracting from them.

Hobby (streaming, The Lab Files, CS2, the game) is not the company face. It lives on [dtrtlab.com/lab.html](https://dtrtlab.com/lab.html).

## Logo usage

Logo: **Concept A, "D/ Refined"** (approved 2026-10-15). A geometric **D** built on a 16-unit grid (outer bowl radius 4.5u, 2u stroke, counter kept open) followed by a **slash** at the 1:3 slope (about 18.4°). Theme A colors are locked: navy `#0B2545`, green `#13C27A`.

- Primary mark (navy tile): `img/brand/mark.svg`. White D, green slash on `#0B2545`. Same file serves `favicon.svg` and `img/icon.svg`.
- Favicons: `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `img/brand/favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `favicon-48.png`. App icons: `apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `img/brand/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` (mark at 0.84 so iOS/Android masks don't clip it).
- Avatar (circle-safe, mark at 0.68): `img/brand/avatar-a.png` (512), `avatar-a-400.png`, `avatar-a-1080.png`, `avatar-a.svg`. Use these for X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch and Whop profile images. `img/brand/dtrt-avatar.png` is the same art at the old URL.
- Share cards (1200×630): `img/brand/og-theme-a.png` (public pages; also copied to `dtrt-social.png`), `img/brand/og-owner-dashboard.png` (`/live/` only).
- Allowed colorways: white D + green slash on navy (default); navy D + green slash on white or `#F4F7FB`; one-color all-white on navy when green can't print. Never black-and-white or orange versions, and never recolor the slash to anything but `#13C27A`.
- Do not change the geometry: no rounding the slash, no reflowing the D, no outlines, gradients, glows or drop shadows, no stretching or rotating.
- Clear space: at least the D's stroke width (2u = 1/8 of the mark) on every side; minimum size 16 px (favicon) or 24 px elsewhere.
- On photos, place the mark on a solid navy or white field, never directly on a busy image.
- Wordmark in the interface: **DTRT** with **THE LAB** set small beside it. Do not restack it into a new logo.
- Source and construction drawings: Brain note `research/logo-geometry-2026-10-15` (private).

## Colors (Theme A, locked 2026-10-15)

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `#0B2545` | Navy. Header, hero and footer bands, dark panels, body text on light backgrounds, logo tile |
| Accent | `#13C27A` | Green. Primary buttons (navy text), the logo slash, highlights, focus rings, eyebrows on navy |
| Text on navy | `#FFFFFF` | Headlines and copy on navy |
| Light | `#DCE6F2` | Light section bands, secondary copy on navy, outline-button hover |
| Page | `#FFFFFF` | Page background and cards |

Contrast: white on navy 15.4:1, `#DCE6F2` on navy 12.2:1, green on navy 6.6:1. Green on white is only 2.3:1, so never set green text or thin green lines on white.

Light-background charts only: use the darker green `#0A8754` (4.6:1 on white) for chart lines, bars and data labels on white or light panels. It is not a brand color: not for buttons, the logo or type.

Buttons are green with navy text, or outlined in the current text color. The old near-black/white palette and orange are retired from the company face (they remain only on the sequestered hobby/stream pages).

## Fonts

- Interface: **Source Sans 3**, used on the site as DTRT Sans. Files: `assets/fonts/source-sans-3-latin.woff2`.
- Long reading text, when a serif is needed: **Source Serif 4**. File: `assets/fonts/source-serif-4-latin.woff2`.
- Fallback: Arial, then sans-serif.
- Do not substitute a display or script face for the wordmark.

## Profile photo and banner

- Profile photo: a real head-and-shoulders photo, soft light, plain or quiet background, no party crop and no other people. Until that photo is supplied, use `img/brand/dtrt-avatar.png`. Do not invent a portrait.
- Banner: a wide still of real work (desk, notes, workshop), not a stock skyline and not a view-count graphic. Keep type white (`#FFFFFF`) on navy (`#0B2545`) if you add words. Safe center for crops. No fake client logos.
- Square posts: the mark or a real work still. Story and reel covers can be vertical crops of the same still.
- Do not use screenshots of private inboxes, IDs, or other people’s faces without consent.
