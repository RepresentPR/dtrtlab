The one-hour gap

17%

Only 17% of HVAC companies reply to a new lead within an hour. 78% reply the same day.

Source: Jobber, 2026 Home Service Trends Report, via Jobber's HVAC industry trends.

Most companies do reply. Just not fast. Jobber also notes that customers often reach out to more than one company at once, and the first business to respond often gets the job.

When you're on a job all day, the office work waits until night. So what if the office work kept up, and you only touched what needs you?

What the Job Desk is

Four screens: Tonight, Needs you, Jobs and a sent log.

The system drafts each customer message, and you approve, edit or skip it. It goes out only after you approve it. Autopilot, which would send without asking, is an opt-in setting, and it's off by default.

Tonight: the nightly Report Card

  • Every night, you get one screen on your phone. It sends at 8 PM by default, and you can pick any time.
  • It starts with one line: all clear, or how many things need you.
  • Then at most 3 things, most urgent first, and 4 numbers compared with last week.
  • One line says what you approved and sent today, linked to the full log.
  • Urgent pings go to you only, for safety words or a lead waiting over an hour, and never more than 2 a day.

Needs you: one queue

The order is safety first, then urgent, then money at stake, then what's due. Any lead waiting over 15 minutes counts as urgent and turns red. In the demo, Maria's web form says "No heat, furnace clicks but won't start," and she has waited 48 minutes, so she's first.

Approve, edit or skip

  • Edit: change a word or rewrite it. It saves as a draft.
  • Approve: only then does it go out, and it leaves the list. (In the demo, nothing is really sent.)
  • Skip: it won't come back tonight.

Jobs: one timeline per job

Jobs shows every job and where it stands. Open one to see what the customer said, what the system drafted, and what you approved and sent.

Open quotes get up to 3 drafted nudges, on day 2, day 5 and day 10, and each one goes out only when you approve it. Drafts stop the moment the customer replies. They count as marketing, so consent must be on file and STOP is always honored. Mark a job won or lost, and the remaining nudges stop.

Sent log: you approve every send

The sent log lists everything that went out today, and every customer message in it reads "Approved by owner · sent." Job cards made from email can be undone. Texts and emails can't be unsent, which is why each one waits for your OK.

Autopilot, which would send without asking, is a separate opt-in setting, and it's off by default. It stays off in the demo. One switch pauses all drafts.

What it never does

  • Touch prices, refunds, invoices or payments. Always you.
  • Give technical or safety advice. Safety words like gas, CO, sparks or flood ping your phone right away.
  • Contact an upset customer. A low score lands in Needs you as "call them."
  • Hold back the review link. Every finished job gets the same one, whatever the score.
