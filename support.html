Before you support

A few clear answers.

Does support buy a service or booking?

No. Support is voluntary. For paid work, discuss a project with us first. We agree the scope, quote and terms with you before any work is booked. Sending an enquiry creates no charge.

Can I start with a free plan?

Yes. Send a business link or brief idea description and one goal. The plan includes three practical priorities, one sample improvement and a clear next step. No purchase required; implementation is scoped separately. Request a free plan.

Can I buy a game or key?

No. Store and preorder checkout are off. There is no public game download.

Where are subscriptions handled?

Twitch and Kick handle their own subscriptions and gifts. DTRT Lab does not collect your card details on this website. Review the platform's terms before choosing an option.

What about a brand partnership?

Explore partnership enquiries. We agree the placement, creative, dates, quote and disclosure before a booking. An enquiry alone does not book a placement.

How else can I help?

Watch a video, try the guide, share something useful, and tell us what you want to see next.

Support is not represented as a tax-deductible charitable donation.
