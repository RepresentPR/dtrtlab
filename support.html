Before you support

A few clear answers.

Is a tip a purchase or a preorder?

No. Tips are voluntary support. They do not buy a game, a key, early access or a sponsorship. The Ko-fi page explains its current funding goal.

Where is the payment handled?

On Ko-fi or the relevant creator platform. DTRT Lab does not collect your card details on this website. Review the amount, currency, fees and renewal terms at the destination.

Can I buy an ad placement?

Start with a partnership enquiry. We'll agree the placement, creative, dates, price and disclosure before a booking or payment. Sending an enquiry creates no charge.

How else can I help?

Watch an episode, follow the channel, share a moment you enjoyed, and tell us what you want to see next.

Support payments are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.
