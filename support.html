Before you support

A few clear answers.

Can I buy a game or key?

No. Store and preorder checkout are off. There is no public game download.

Where are subscriptions handled?

Twitch and Kick handle their own subscriptions and gifts. DTRT Lab does not collect your card details on this website. Review the amount, currency, fees and renewal terms on the platform.

Can I buy an ad placement?

Start with a partnership enquiry. We'll agree the placement, creative, dates, price and disclosure before a booking or payment. Sending an enquiry creates no charge.

How else can I help?

Watch an episode, follow the channel, share a moment you enjoyed, and tell us what you want to see next.

Support payments are not represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.
