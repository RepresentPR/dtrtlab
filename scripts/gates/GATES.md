- **Term lists:** banned terms are stored only as hashes, using the same scheme as `scripts/private-terms.test.cjs`. That file's lists are read at run time. Fix the copy, never the lists. List changes go through Brain and QA. The 11/27 marketplace amendment (Brain §3) allows the island's name only on marketplace pages (mercado and store, the home store section, and their ES versions) and on fiction pages; it is flagged everywhere else. Personal town names and addresses stay banned. MOTION official wording (matched case-insensitively and with whitespace collapsed) or the affiliate link requires the exact opener `Affiliate link. We earn a commission.` (or the approved Spanish opener). The official wording trigger ignores case and extra spaces; the opener itself stays exact. A nearby negation only exempts an AI-is-live claim when it denies liveness itself in the same sentence (for example `not live yet`, `isn't live`, `not connected`, `does not activate`, or `has never been live`); an unrelated `not on sale` or `not up for debate`, or a denial in the next sentence (`Try our live AI. Not connected yet.`), does not. The live+AI bigram does not match across a sentence end (`. ! ? : ;`, em dash, or emoji), a bullet, a middle-dot, or a line/list break. List-marker breaks (`-`, `*`, `+`, `1.`) count only at a real line start (after a newline plus optional indent), never mid-line, so `live - AI` and `live 1. AI` still fail. Singular `my kid` and Spanish `mi hijo` / `mi hija` are banned. Spanish liveness claims (`IA en vivo`, `IA en directo`, `IA ya disponible`) are caught, as are copula forms: IA / `la IA` / `nuestra IA` (and gender, number, and `el AI` variants) + optional ya/ahora + está/es/estará + optional ya/ahora + en vivo / en directo / disponible / activa / funcionando / lista. English mirrors that with AI + is/'s/now + live/available/active/ready/online, so `Our AI is now available` fails. A Spanish denial only exempts when it negates liveness itself in the same sentence (`no está en vivo`, `todavía no`, `aún no`, `no está activa`, `no funciona todavía`, `nunca ha estado en vivo`); an unrelated `no un truco` or a `no` in the next sentence does not. An English denial after a claim counts only when it negates the AI claim itself in the same clause, with AI or `it` as the subject (`is not available`, `..., it is not ready`). `Try our live AI, not available in red.`, `not ready for merch preorders`, and `not online shopping` do not exempt.
- **CI:** `.github/workflows/gates.yml` runs `scripts/gates/run_ci.sh` on every PR and push to main. It runs the gate unit tests, `copy_lint.py` on every tracked `DRAFTS/`, `drafts/` or `captions/` file, and `done_gate.py` on every tracked PROGRESS.md, AUTOPILOT-*.md, QA-NOTE-*.md or open-issues.md.
- **Box copies:** box copies under `/home/box/dtrt-checks/gates/` are convenience mirrors only. The repo copy is the source of truth. To refresh the mirror, copy from a fresh checkout of main. A mirrored `copy_lint.py` needs `--terms <repo>/scripts/private-terms.test.cjs`. Without the repo lists it refuses to run (exit 2) instead of passing.
