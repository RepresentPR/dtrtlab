METHOD / DAILY PRACTICE IMPROVEMENT DISCIPLINE

The one percent rule.

Make one useful improvement attempt. Record what changed and what the evidence says—even when the result is flat, worse, or unknown.

How progress is recorded

Each note needs a date, baseline, actual observation, source and next step. One percent is the name of the discipline; it is not a measured growth rate or a guarantee.

Public results are reviewed and redacted. Private requests and raw conversations stay private.

LEARNING / OPEN QUESTION UNVERIFIED

A working page is only one part of a business.

What a result needs

Success or failure is recorded against a stated criterion and dated observation. No customer, revenue, world-ranking or always-on claim is made by this notebook.
