Privacy

Last updated October 11, 2026. This describes how dtrtlab.com works today. Contact DTRTCollision@outlook.com with a privacy question or a request about a message you sent.

Local featuresThe CS2 crosshair preview and eDPI calculator run in your browser. The community board, accounts, votes, notes, and provider-key connections are disabled on this public site. There is no public game download or cloud score service.
ContactContact and partnership forms prepare a message in your email app; you review and send it there. We use messages we receive to respond and handle the inquiry. Email is handled by the mail providers.
SupportTips go through Ko-fi. We do not take card numbers or PayPal passwords on this site.
Cookies and storageNo DTRT Lab cookie is required to read the site. An analytics choice may be saved in your browser. If you allow Google Analytics, Google may set cookies. External players may use their own storage. Heatmaps and session replay are not used.
PlausibleCookieless counts on dtrtlab.com cover pages, referrers, device categories, outbound links, downloads, and named link or form-intent clicks. Form text is not sent as an analytics event. No ad-network script or sale of visitor data is used.
Google AnalyticsOptional. Its script loads only after you select Allow Google. It is used for visit, page, source, and event reports. The site configures IP anonymization and turns off Google Signals and ad personalization. See the Google Privacy Policy.
Analytics choicesSelect No Google cookies to decline Google Analytics. You can change or withdraw a saved Google Analytics choice below. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, this site loads neither Plausible nor Google Analytics. These choices do not prevent external players or font services from loading on pages that use them.
External servicesThe live showcase embeds a Twitch player and chat. Some other pages load Twitch or YouTube players after interaction or when a stream is live, and some query DecAPI or Kick for public stream status. The CS2 and tab pages load Google Fonts. Those services may receive your IP address and browser information and may use their own storage under their policies. Following a social, support, or other external link takes you to that service.
Search ConsoleIf connected, Google already has search queries as the search engine. We may view impressions, clicks, and queries. That is not a script on this page.
SaleNo. We do not sell personal information.
