SAMPLE FREE PLAN · FICTIONAL EXAMPLE

This is what you get back.
One link. One goal. One plan.

Café Ejemplo is a made-up business. The page and wording below are illustrations, not a client result.

What they sent

Link: their café homepage. Goal: more weekday breakfast pickup orders.

Three practical priorities

  1. Say what you sell in the first line. The page opens with “Welcome to our family since 2019.” A hungry visitor still doesn’t know you do fast breakfast.
  2. Give one clear next step. Menu, About, Gallery and Events all compete. Put one button first: Order for pickup.
  3. Show proof you already have. Their best reviews sit on another site. Place two or three real ones, used with permission, beside the order button.

One sample improvement

Before

Welcome to our family since 2019. We love coffee, community and good vibes.

After

Breakfast sandwiches and local coffee, ready in 5 minutes.
[ Order for pickup ]

A clear next step

Change the first line and the button this week. Then compare weekday pickup orders for two weeks against the two weeks before. Keep it if it helps; try the next priority if it doesn’t.

What we look at on every plan

  • Can a stranger tell what you do in five seconds?
  • Is there one obvious next step?
  • Is the offer clear: what it is, who it’s for, and how to ask?
  • Is real proof visible near the decision?
  • Does it work on a phone?
  • Is it easy to contact you?

A free plan reads your public page and your goal. It does not access your accounts, measure your traffic, or promise results.
