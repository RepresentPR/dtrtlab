Fact sheet

DTRT Lab

An original offline first-bullet laboratory: move, stop, acquire, fire — UNIT 12 drops only on a credited stopped head.

Creator: Ian Ferraro Crespo. Engine: Godot 4.7. Platform: Windows 10/11 64-bit (release candidate). Players: 1. Account: none. Store: not live.

50 words. DTRT Lab is a premium PC performance-training game, not a CS2 overlay. AIM builds the mouse. DTRT Lab applies it under movement. Walking shots never count. The coach picks TRAIN THIS NEXT from your samples. Worlds are original Puerto Rican-inspired places — Añasco is home. No account. No injection. No loot.

FAQ

What is DTRT?

Do The Right Thing. A first-bullet lab. Stop, then fire.

Is this a game?

Yes. A training game with a world, a coach, and a loop — not a spreadsheet.

Is it an aim trainer?

It contains AIM. The product is AIM plus the stop. Dummy drops are the dopamine, not plate scores.

Does it connect to CS2?

No.

Does it modify CS2?

No. It does not read memory, inject, or bypass anti-cheat.

Does it require an account?

No. Progress is local.

What platforms?

Windows RC. Play.bat launches the editor binary against the project until export templates exist.

What is the movement system?

CS2-inspired Source acceleration at 128 ticks. Documented. Not a 1:1 claim. Keys rebind.

System requirements (draft)

Tested on Windows with D3D12. Not a lab report.

OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit. CPU: 4 cores. RAM: 8 GB. GPU: Direct3D 12, 4 GB (Ultra wants more). Storage: about 1 GB. Mouse + keyboard. Displays: 1080p–1440p; 144 Hz and up preferred.
