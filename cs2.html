One crosshair, four hours.

09 // Your key

        
An account DTRT Lab cannot open.

        
The password encrypts a signing key in this browser. It is not sent, not stored on the server, and there is no reset. Ian cannot sign in as you, read the password, or recover it. Other people see the display name on votes and notes. If you forget the password, the account is gone.
